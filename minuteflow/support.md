# Help — MinuteFlow

MinuteFlow records meetings on your iPhone and turns them into a transcript, notes and action
items. It is in private testing: the AI features need an invite code.

## Getting started

1. Open MinuteFlow and follow the welcome steps.
2. Enter your invite code to connect to the processing server.
3. Google Drive is optional: connect it, or skip it and keep everything on your iPhone.

## Questions

**Does it keep recording when the screen locks?**
Yes. Recording continues in the background until you stop it. A phone call pauses it, and you can
continue in the same recording afterwards.

**Can it record phone calls or video calls in other apps?**
No. iOS does not let apps record calls or other apps' audio. A call on speakerphone next to the
iPhone can be recorded with the microphone.

**Which languages does it understand?**
English, Spanish, Hungarian, Italian, French, German and Portuguese, also mixed in one meeting.
Notes can be written in any of them.

**Is anything shared automatically?**
No. Nothing is shared or uploaded when a recording ends. You choose what to share, review it, and
confirm.

**Do I need Google Drive?**
No. Without it, share with the iOS Share Sheet (Mail, WhatsApp, Messages, AirDrop, Files…). If
you connect Drive, MinuteFlow can only see the files it creates, and you can disconnect it in
Settings → Google Drive.

**How do I delete everything?**
Settings → Privacy & data → Delete all MinuteFlow data. Deleting the app also removes it.

**How are the AI notes made, and can they be wrong?**
They are written by Google's Gemini AI. AI can make mistakes: MinuteFlow checks quotes, owners and
deadlines against the transcript and flags anything it could not verify. Check what matters
against the recording.
