Features

Help with homework, without the guesswork.

From a photo of the page to an explanation your child understands.

Snap the homework

Take a photo or pick one from your library, up to 5 pages at a time.

Reads real handwriting

Handwritten and printed exercises, with the subject and the maths method the school uses.

Right, wrong and why

Every answer is marked on the sheet itself, with a clear explanation for each mistake.

Ask it another way

Something unclear? Ask the assistant and it explains it differently.

A profile for each child

Avatars instead of photos, history and progress by subject, streaks and achievements.

Private by default

No sign-up, no password and no ads. Children's names stay on your phone and homework photos are not kept.
