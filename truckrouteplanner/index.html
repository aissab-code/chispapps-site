Features

Built for the rules professional drivers work under.

Plan the whole trip before the engine starts.

Driving times and breaks

45-minute breaks and daily rests under Regulation (EC) No 561/2006, including split breaks and reduced daily rests.

Stop plan

The time and kilometre of every mandatory break and rest, for one or two drivers.

Tolls, fuel and cost

Estimated tolls by country from weight, axles and EURO class, plus fuel and total cost per kilometre.

National rules

Driving bans, low-emission zones and winter equipment for every country on the route.

Map and alerts

Route map with the stops, and a reminder on your phone before each mandatory break.

Report and GPX

A text report of the plan and GPX export for your navigation device.

Results are planning estimates and are not legally binding. Always check current traffic restrictions, toll rates and vehicle limits, and your actual tachograph records. This is not a government app; see the official sources we use.
