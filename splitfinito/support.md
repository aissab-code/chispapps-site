# Help — SplitFinito

A question, a problem, or something to report? Email **aissa.b.code@gmail.com**. We answer reports of
abusive content within 24 hours and everything else as soon as we can.

Other languages: [Español](soporte) · [Français](aide) · [Deutsch](hilfe) · [Italiano](assistenza) · [Português](ajuda)

## Getting started

Open the app, choose a name and an avatar, and create a group (a trip, a flat, a couple or anything
else). Tap **Invite** to send the link to your WhatsApp group or show the QR code. No one needs an
account, an email address or a password.

## My friend doesn't have the app yet

Add them by name: they become a member like anyone else, and you can add expenses for them straight
away. When they install SplitFinito and open the invite link, they choose "That's me" on their name and
take over the seat with all its history.

## How do friends join?

They tap the invite link. If the app is installed it opens directly. If not, the page shows the store
buttons: install the app, then tap the link again, or copy it and paste it in the app (*Scan → Paste*).

## Where are my expenses stored?

On your phone and on the phone of every member of the group: everyone keeps the full record. Changes
travel between phones through an encrypted mailbox that cannot read them and deletes them once
delivered. We have no copy. Details in the [Privacy Policy](privacy).

## A friend doesn't see my expense

Changes are delivered when the phones are online, usually within seconds. Check the *Last synced* line at
the top of the group and pull down to refresh. Rows with a small dot are still waiting to be sent. If the
mailbox can't be reached, open the expense and use **Share → Send as link** in your group chat: the link
carries the encrypted cost and adds it to your friends' phones when they tap it.

## I changed or lost my phone

- If you exported a backup (*Account → Backup and data → Export backup*), install the app and choose
  **Restore from a backup**.
- Otherwise, ask a friend for the group's invite link, join, and pick your old name under "Is this
  you?". Your friends' phones send you the whole history.

## Currencies

Each group has a base currency. You can enter any expense in another currency: the app suggests the day's
rate (from free public sources) and saves it in the expense, so everyone sees the same numbers. You can
change the rate by hand.

## Simplified debts

With *Simplify debts* on, SplitFinito suggests the fewest payments needed to settle the group. Nobody's
total changes, only who pays whom. Tap **Why?** on any suggested payment to see the expenses behind it,
or switch to the detailed view.

## Settling up

Tap **Settle up**, choose the payment and mark it as paid. If the person shared their payment details,
buttons can open your payment app with the amount, or copy the details. SplitFinito never moves money
itself. The person who receives the money can confirm the payment.

## Notifications

Notifications are optional; the app updates every time you open it. In *Account → Notifications* you can
turn off **Show details** so that notifications only say "There is new activity in one of your groups",
or mute single groups.

## My invite link was shared with the wrong people

Open the group, go to *Group settings → Reset invite link*. Old links stop working for new phones.
Remove anyone who should not be in the group from the member list.

## Leave or delete a group

- **Leave group** (in the group settings) removes you from the group on everyone's phone. Your balance
  stays visible to the others until it is settled.
- **Remove from this phone** deletes the group only from your phone. Nobody can delete a group from
  other people's phones.

## Report abusive content or block someone

Press and hold the expense, comment or member and choose **Report**: the app prepares an email to us.
Choose **Block** on a member to hide everything they write and ignore their changes on your phone. Any
member can also remove someone from the group. See section 7 of the [Terms of Use](terms).

## Delete my data

*Account → Backup and data → Delete my data* erases everything on your phone and removes your phone's
records from our mailbox. Details in the [Privacy Policy](privacy#delete).

## Is SplitFinito really free?

Yes. No ads, no subscription, no premium features and no limits on how many expenses you add. It runs on
free infrastructure and keeps your data on your phones instead of on our servers.
