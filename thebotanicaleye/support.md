# The Botanical Eye — Help

Questions, a bug, or trouble with your subscription? Email **aissa.b.code@gmail.com** — we
usually answer within 1–2 business days. Include **your ID** from Profile › Your ID if it is
about your access or your data.

## Frequently asked questions

**How do I scan a plant?**
Open the app, tap **Scan a plant** and point the camera at the plant. You get an
identification, a health diagnosis and care tips. Fill the frame with leaves or flowers, in
good light, and keep other things — people, documents — out of the picture. The first time,
the app asks your permission to send the photo to the artificial intelligence that analyses it.

**How many free scans do I get?**
A set number every week; the app shows how many are left. Premium removes the limit.

**What does Premium include, and how much is it?**
Unlimited scans and the full set of care features. The current price, in your currency, is on
the upgrade screen (Profile › Upgrade to Premium).

**I have a code. Where do I enter it?**
Codes are redeemed in the store, not inside the app. Open Profile › Upgrade to Premium and tap
**Have a promo code?** On iPhone that opens Apple's code sheet; on Android it opens Google
Play's redeem page. You can also redeem on Google Play directly: Play Store › your profile ›
Payments & subscriptions › Redeem code.

**How do I cancel?**
- **iPhone:** Settings › your name › Subscriptions › The Botanical Eye.
- **Android:** Play Store › your profile › Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions.

Cancelling stops future renewals; Premium stays until the end of the period you paid for.

**I paid but Premium isn't active.**
On the upgrade screen tap **Manage Subscription** and restore your purchases, using the same
Apple ID or Google account you paid with. Still stuck? Email us with your ID.

**Where is "Your ID", and what is it for?**
Profile › Your ID. It is a random code for your installation. Send it to us if we have offered
you free access, or with any request about your data. Reinstalling the app creates a new one.

**Is the identification always right?**
No — it is generated by AI and can be wrong, especially with poor light or look-alike species.
Never use it to decide whether a plant is safe to eat or to keep near children or pets.

**What happens to my photos?**
Scanned photos are analysed and not kept by us. Photos you save to your garden stay on your
phone. Details: [Privacy policy](privacy) · [Terms of use](terms).
