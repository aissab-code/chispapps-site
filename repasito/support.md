# Help — Repasito

A question, or something not working? Email **aissa.b.code@gmail.com** and we will get back
to you as soon as we can.

## How do I check homework?

Open **Scan**, take a photo of each page (up to 5 per check) or pick it from your photos with
🖼️, and tap **✓ Analyse**. The first time, Repasito asks your permission to send the photos to
the artificial intelligence that checks them.

## Tips for a good result

- Good light, with the whole sheet flat and straight in the photo.
- One page per photo.
- If the handwriting is hard to read, leave **🧠 Enhance writing** on.
- The AI can make mistakes: check anything that looks odd. Counting many small objects is
  what it does worst.

## How many pages can I check?

- **Free:** 10 pages a week. The count resets every Monday.
- **Repasito Plus:** up to 200 pages a week. The yearly plan starts with 7 days free.

## How do I manage or cancel Repasito Plus?

On your iPhone: **Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Repasito**. On Android: open the
**Play Store → your profile picture → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Repasito**.
Or in the app: **Profile → Manage subscription**.

## I changed phones and can't see my subscription

Use the same Apple ID (iPhone) or Google account (Android), open **Profile → Upgrade to Plus**
and tap **Restore purchases**.

## I have a promo code

Tap **Promo code** on the Home screen. On iPhone, Apple's redemption sheet opens for you to
enter it; you can also redeem it in the App Store by tapping your profile picture → **Redeem
Gift Card or Code**. On Android, type it in the screen that opens; a Google Play code can also
be redeemed in the Play Store: your profile picture → **Payments & subscriptions → Redeem
code**.

## What happens to my data?

Your children's profiles stay on your phone only. Homework photos are sent to the AI only to
check them, and are not kept. The details are in the [privacy policy](privacy). To have your
data deleted, email aissa.b.code@gmail.com.
