# Privacy Policy — Ludora

**Last updated:** 15 October 2026

Ludora is a collection of single-player puzzle games for Android and iOS, published by **Aissa Apps**.
This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

## 1. Who is responsible

- **Controller:** Aissa Bamogo Redondo (sole trader, Spain), trading as Aissa Apps
- **Contact:** aissa.b.code@gmail.com

## 2. The short version

Ludora does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal data. There are no
accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Everything stays on your device.

## 3. What is stored, and where

To let you resume games and keep your records, Ludora saves the following **only on your device**,
in the app's private storage:

- your settings (music, sound effects, haptics, motion and game options);
- your progress: experience points, level, achievements, unlocked styles, daily challenge history
  and streaks;
- your personal bests and statistics for each game;
- any game you left unfinished, so it can be resumed.

This information never leaves your device. We cannot see it and have no copy of it.

## 4. What we do not do

- We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or any other contact details.
- We do not use advertising identifiers, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking services.
- We do not use your location, camera, microphone, contacts or files.
- We do not show ads and there are no in-app purchases.
- The app does not send any data over the internet.

## 5. Permissions

Ludora uses vibration for optional haptic feedback and standard audio settings to play its sounds
and music. Both can be turned off in the app's Settings. Other permissions in the Android build are
standard ones added by the app framework; none is used to collect or send data.

## 6. Deleting your data

You can erase everything at any time in **Settings → Reset all progress**, or by uninstalling the
app. Because we hold no data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

## 7. Children

Ludora is a general-audience puzzle game and does not knowingly collect information from anyone,
including children.

## 8. Your rights

Because Ludora processes no personal data on our side, there is nothing for us to access, correct,
export or erase. If you have a question or concern, write to aissa.b.code@gmail.com. You can also
contact your data protection authority (in Spain, the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos,
[aepd.es](https://www.aepd.es)).

## 9. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date.
