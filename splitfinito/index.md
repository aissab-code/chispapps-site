# SplitFinito

**Split it. Finito.** Share the costs of trips, flats and nights out with your friends. Free, no
account, no daily limits, no ads, and it works offline.

*Divídelo. Finito. · Partagez. Finito. · Teilen. Finito. · Dividi. Finito. · Divide. Finito.*

## Why SplitFinito

- **No account.** Pick a name and start. Friends join from a WhatsApp link or a QR code.
- **No limits, no ads, no subscription.** Every feature is free: currencies, charts, receipts, exports.
- **Every cost is kept on every friend's phone.** There is no central database: each member keeps the
  complete record of the group, and changes travel end-to-end encrypted.
- **Works offline.** Add expenses anywhere; they are delivered when you are back online.
- **Settle in fewer payments,** with a "Why?" behind every suggested payment.
- **Friends without the app** can be added by name and take over their seat later.
