Partner programme terms

Version 1 · 9 October 2026

These terms govern the collaboration between Aissa Bamogo Redondo, sole trader in Spain, owner of the app The Botanical Eye ("the app", "we"), and the person taking part in the programme ("you"). You accept them by ticking them on the application form or by using your code. If a Spanish version exists, the two have the same content; in case of doubt the Spanish version prevails.

1. What your followers get

Your personal code gives 1 month of Premium free to anyone subscribing for the first time with it. It is run by Apple (App Store) and Google (Google Play), so their rules also apply: for example, it only works for people who have not subscribed before, and each person can redeem it once.

2. What you earn

You earn 20% of the net revenue of every period paid by the people who redeem your code, during the 12 months after they redeem it.

Net revenue is what the app actually receives: the price paid minus VAT and minus Apple's or Google's commission, as estimated by those stores for each transaction.

The free month earns nothing, and nor do periods paid after those 12 months.

3. When and how you are paid

  • Each commission is held for 30 days from the payment, in case of a refund.
  • After that it becomes "ready to pay". We pay at the end of each month, by bank transfer, once your ready-to-pay balance reaches €20; otherwise it carries over to the next month.
  • We need an invoice from you for each payment. Taxes on your income are your responsibility.

4. Refunds

If Apple or Google refund a payment that earned you commission, that commission is deducted from your next payout. Your dashboard shows it as "Refund".

5. What we ask of you

  • Always label the collaboration as advertising, clearly and from the start (for example #ad or "Advertising"), as the law requires.
  • Describe the app honestly. It is an AI-powered aid: it can misidentify a plant and does not replace a professional. Never claim it can tell whether a plant is edible or safe for children or pets.
  • Address adults. Never minors.
  • No spam. Do not send unsolicited messages or use bots, fake accounts or code-sharing networks.
  • Do not use your own code yourself or on accounts you control, and do not bid on "The Botanical Eye" or "El Ojo Botánico" in paid ads.

6. Your dashboard

You get a private link with your figures: how many people used your code, how many pay, and your commissions. It is personal: do not share it. If it leaks by mistake, ask us for a new one and the old link stops working.

7. Personal data

You only ever see totals, never who used your code. We process your name, email, public profile and the details needed to pay you and meet our tax obligations, solely to run this collaboration. You can exercise your rights by writing to aissa.b.code@gmail.com.

8. Duration and ending

  • You or we can end the collaboration at any time by written notice.
  • When it ends, we pay the commission on periods paid before the end date, except in case of fraud or a serious breach of these terms.
  • We may update these terms with 30 days' notice. If you disagree, you can end the collaboration before they take effect.
