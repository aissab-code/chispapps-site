# What's new in SplitFinito

Real release notes, not "various fixes and improvements". Newest first.

## 1.0.0 — first release

- Groups for trips, homes, couples and anything else, plus a personal group for your own spending.
- No account: choose a name and an avatar and start. Invite friends with a link or a QR code.
- Friends without the app can be added by name and claim their seat later with the whole history.
- Every split you need: equally, by amounts, percentages, shares, adjustments, item by item, and
  refunds. Several people can pay one expense.
- Any currency on any expense, with the day's rate saved in the expense; balances in the group currency.
- Balances in two views: simplified payments with a "Why?" for each, and who actually paid whom.
- Settle up in one tap, with shortcuts to open your payment app or copy payment details.
- Recurring expenses, templates, receipt photos, comments and a full history where everything can be
  undone.
- Totals by month, category, person and day; CSV, JSON and PDF exports; encrypted backups.
- Works offline. Every change is end-to-end encrypted and kept on every member's phone.
- Dark mode, Face ID or fingerprint lock, and six languages: English, Spanish, French, German, Italian
  and Portuguese.
