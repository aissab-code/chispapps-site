# Help — Ludora

A question, or something not working? Email **aissa.b.code@gmail.com** and we will get back to you
as soon as we can.

## Where is my progress saved?

On your phone only. Ludora has no accounts and no servers, so progress isn't synced between devices
and is lost if you uninstall the app.

## How do I resume a game?

Unfinished games are saved automatically. Open the Lounge and tap **Resume** under *Continue*, or
open the game and choose Resume.

## How do scores, XP and levels work?

Each game keeps its own score and personal bests. XP is shared across all games and fills your level.
Repeating the same game and mode many times in one day earns less XP. The details are in the app
under **Settings → How scoring and XP work**.

## What are daily challenges?

Every day there is one challenge per game — the same puzzle for everyone. Completing one earns
bonus XP and keeps your streak going. Missing a day only resets the streak; nothing else is lost.

## How do I turn off music, sounds or vibration?

Open **Settings** (the gear on the Lounge), or pause any game. Each one has its own switch. You can
also reduce animations there.

## How do I start over?

**Settings → Reset all progress** erases your XP, achievements, statistics, styles and saved games
on this device.
