# Privacy Policy — Repasito

**Last updated:** 14 October 2026

This policy explains what Repasito does with personal data. Repasito is a homework-checking
app used at home by a **parent or legal guardian** to support a child's learning.

We comply with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (**GDPR**), the EU Artificial
Intelligence Act (**AI Act**) and, where applicable, the U.S. Children's Online Privacy
Protection Act (**COPPA**).

---

## 1. Who is responsible for your data

- **Controller:** Aissa Bamogo Redondo (sole trader, Spain)
- **Contact:** aissa.b.code@gmail.com

---

## 2. Who should use Repasito

Repasito is designed to be used at home by an **adult** with parental responsibility. By
using the app you confirm that you are at least 18 years old and that you are the parent or
legal guardian of any child whose homework you scan.

Repasito is a home study aid. It is **not intended for use by schools, teachers or other
educational institutions** to grade, assess, admit, place or rank pupils, nor for any
official evaluation of a child's learning.

The app is **not directed to children under 13** for independent use. A child's work appears
in the app because checking it is the point of the app, but the account and every control in
it belong to the adult.

---

## 3. You do not create an account

Repasito has no sign-up, no login and no password. On first launch the app creates an
**anonymous session**, which is a random identifier used to keep your scan count and your
subscription status attached to your installation.

On iPhone, a copy of the session's renewal token is kept in the iOS Keychain on your phone,
which iOS keeps when an app is deleted. If you reinstall Repasito, the app continues the same
anonymous session, so your Repasito ID, your pages and your subscription stay attached.

We never ask for, and never receive, your name, your email address or your phone number.

---

## 4. What stays on your phone

The following never leaves your device:

- **Your children's profiles** — the name you type and the avatar you pick. These are stored
  in the app's local storage on your phone and are never uploaded to our servers.
- Your language, onboarding status and other app preferences.
- A local cache of your recent scan summaries.

Repasito does **not** let you attach a photograph of a child to a profile. Profiles use a
fixed set of illustrated avatars instead. If you used an older version that stored such a
photo, the app deletes it automatically when you update.

Everything in this section is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its storage.

---

## 5. What we store on our servers

Our backend runs on Supabase. Attached to your anonymous identifier we store:

- **Usage and plan data:** your weekly page counter, the current week, whether you have an
  active subscription and when it expires, any promo code you redeem, and your language.
- **Scan results:** for each scan, the subject, the score, how many answers were correct,
  incorrect or partial, and the **structured analysis returned by the AI**. That analysis
  contains a transcription of the exercises and the explanations shown to you, so it may
  include whatever text was written on the page.
- **Partner codes:** if you redeem a partner's code — for example an influencer's
  "1 month free" code — we record which partner it was, so that we can pay them a commission
  on your subscription for up to 12 months. The partner only ever sees totals: how many
  people used their code and what they have earned. Never who you are.

We do **not** store the homework photograph itself. We do **not** store your children's
names — those stay on your phone, as described above.

---

## 6. Artificial intelligence

Repasito uses artificial intelligence — **Google's Gemini models** — to read homework, check
answers, write explanations and answer the follow-up questions you ask in the app.

- **Every correction, score, explanation and chat answer is generated by AI**, not by a
  person. No human reviews them before you see them.
- **AI can make mistakes.** It is least reliable with unclear handwriting and with counting
  many small objects, and it can be wrong while sounding confident. Treat its corrections as
  support for you, check anything that looks wrong, and do not use them as the only basis for
  decisions about your child's education.
- **No automated decisions about you or your child.** Repasito does not make decisions with
  legal or similarly significant effects (GDPR Art. 22). What to do with a correction is
  always your decision.
- We do **not** use your homework, results or questions to train AI models.

---

## 7. Homework images and AI analysis

When you scan homework, the image is sent from the app to our server, which forwards it to
**Google's Gemini API** for analysis and returns the structured result to you. The image is
held only for the duration of that request and is never written to storage.

Google processes the image under its own terms:
<https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms> and <https://policies.google.com/privacy>.

> **Worth knowing as a parent:** if the page shows your child's handwritten name, their
> school, or anything else identifying, that appears in the image sent to Google and may
> appear in the stored analysis text. Cover or crop it first if you would rather it did not.

## 11. How long we keep it

- **Usage, plan and scan results:** until you ask us to delete them.
- **Homework images:** not kept at all — only for the seconds the analysis takes.
- **Backups:** deleted data disappears from rolling backups within 30 days.
- **Data on your phone:** until you clear the app's storage or uninstall it.

## 12. Your rights

Under GDPR you can ask us to give you a copy of your data, correct it, delete it, restrict
or object to how we use it, or provide it in a portable format. You can also withdraw
consent at any time.

To exercise any of these, email **aissa.b.code@gmail.com**. We answer within 30 days.

Because the app has no login, please include your **Repasito ID** (in the app: Profile → Your
Repasito ID) so we can identify the right records. Step-by-step instructions for deleting your
data are on [this page](delete-data).

You can remove everything held on your device at any time by uninstalling the app or
clearing its storage. That does not remove the server-side records, which we delete on
request as above.

You may also complain to the Spanish data protection authority, the **AEPD**
(<https://www.aepd.es>), or to your local supervisory authority.

## 13. Children's data (COPPA)

Repasito is for adult operators. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children
under 13 for commercial purposes, we show no targeted advertising, and we do not track
anyone across apps or websites.

If you believe we hold data about a child under 13 without parental consent, email
**aissa.b.code@gmail.com** and we will delete it promptly.

## 14. Security

- TLS encryption for everything in transit.
- Row-Level Security in the database, so one installation cannot read another's records.
- All credentials held server-side; none are readable from the app.
- No analytics or advertising SDKs.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your data we will notify you and the
supervisory authority within 72 hours, as GDPR Art. 33 requires.
